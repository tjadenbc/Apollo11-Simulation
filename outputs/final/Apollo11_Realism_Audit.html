Faithfulness guardrail

1969 as-flown — no forward contamination

Nothing post-Apollo-11 may contaminate the simulation via the sibling Artemis I work. Every port is screened by a nature-vs-artifact test — the sim behaves as the real vehicle and crew did wherever 1969 physics and data allow, diverging only where the record genuinely runs out.

Nature ports freely

Gravity fields, ephemerides, time standards, and atmosphere physics describe the same universe Apollo flew through — the best available model wins regardless of publication date. Where 1969-specific data exists (the AS-506 launch-day sounding), it is preferred over a generic standard.

Artifact never ports

Vehicle constants, guidance and navigation laws, dispersion magnitudes, and failure rates must be Apollo-as-flown, sourced to Apollo-era documentation (GSOP / Colossus, TN D-6725, the AS-506 flight-evaluation report, the mission reports). Ascent stays IGM; entry is Apollo's own — not Orion PredGuid.
